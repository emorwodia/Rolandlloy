About Us

Who We Are – Who The Company

Roland Lloyd’s Agencies Limited (RLA) is a professional maritime consulting and service company that delivers diversified Technical/Engineering Services, Marine Warranty Survey, Marine Hull/Machinery Survey and Valuation, Trip-in-Tow Survey, Oil & Gas Inspection, Vessel Chartering Services, Oil Rig Survey, Construction All Risk/Survey on Plants & Machinery, Cargo Superintending/Survey, Health, Safety & Environment (HSE) Services, Training & Certification, among others.
Our business came into being in 1986 and was formally incorporated in 2013. Roland Lloyd’s Agencies Ltd is unique on the ground that she protects the interest of her principal, issues reports without prejudices, and carries out services with the unmatched speed and reliability of a cheetah, making responsiveness one of our hallmarks.
We operate with a clear commitment to technical excellence, regulatory compliance, and ethical business practices. Our multidisciplinary team of professionals brings decades of experience and international exposure, enabling us to meet the evolving needs of clients across sectors such as maritime, oil & gas, construction, energy, and insurance. At RLA, we combine deep local knowledge with global standards to provide end-to-end solutions that are timely, objective, and value-driven. We maintain strategic partnerships, embrace innovation, and invest in continuous capacity development to ensure we remain ahead of industry trends.
With operations headquartered in Nigeria and capacity to serve clients across West Africa, we act as trusted partners—delivering insights, safeguarding assets, and supporting decision-making across every stage of a project or transaction.
